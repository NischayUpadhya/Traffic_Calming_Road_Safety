## 3. Target sheet – A3 Landscape
- **Paper size**: 420 000 mm (width) × 297 000 mm (height).
- **Printable margin**: 10 000 mm on all sides → usable area **X [10 000, 410 000]**, **Y [10 000, 287 000]**.
- **Conceptual scale**: 1 : 100 (i.e., 1 mm on sheet = 100 mm in model).  The detail group already represents the model at full size, so we keep the geometry unchanged and place it directly inside the printable area.

## 4. Placement strategy (translation only)
To keep the internal geometry untouched we will **translate** the whole 55‑entity group.  Align the lower‑left corner of the group with the printable‑area lower‑left corner (10 000, 10 000):

- **ΔX** = 10 000 – 127 000 = **‑117 000 mm**
- **ΔY** = 10 000 – 182 000 = **‑172 000 mm**

After this shift the group occupies:
- X [10 000, 218 000]
- Y [10 000, 173 000]
which comfortably fits inside the printable area and leaves ample space for title‑block, legend, north‑arrow and scale bar.

## 6. Verification checklist (read‑only until DXF is generated)
1. **Extent check** – After applying ΔX/ΔY, the enlarged‑detail group stays within X [10 000, 410 000] and Y [10 000, 287 000].
2. **Collision check** – No overlapping geometry with the newly added title‑block, legend, north‑arrow or border polyline (verify using `ezdxf` `entity.intersect()` or a simple polygon‑intersection routine).
3. **Text readability** – All text heights ≥ 2 mm; at 1:100 this yields ≥ 200 mm on the model, easily readable.
4. **Line‑weight check** – Use widths 0.25 mm (thin), 0.35 mm (medium), 0.5 mm (thick) as per standard A‑scale drafting conventions.
5. **Print preview** – Open the resulting DXF in a CAD viewer, switch to paper‑space, and confirm that no geometry is clipped by the printer margins.
